AI is a tool. Experience is what makes it work.

I use modern AI tools to speed up the routine parts of building software, which keeps your costs down. But anyone can use AI. What it can't do is know what to build, how the pieces should fit together, or what will still hold up a year from now. That takes experience building real, working systems, and I've been doing it for over 20 years.

What the AI helps with

  • Boilerplate and repetitive code
  • First drafts I then shape and refine
  • Repetitive tests and checks

What I bring

  • Understanding how your business actually works
  • Designing a system that fits, and grows with you
  • Reviewing and testing every piece before it ships
  • Knowing what not to build

The result is well-designed, well-thought-out software at a price that makes sense for a small business. And I'm careful with your data: I'll always tell you up front how it's handled.
