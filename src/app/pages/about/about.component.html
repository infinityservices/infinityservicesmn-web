About me

I'm Jeremy, and Infinity Services is me.

I grew up on a small hobby farm, and I've spent more than 20 years working in IT. Over that time I've become something of a jack of all trades, with full-stack development at the center of it. I've always been the person people turn to when there's a new challenge, and I've always wanted to learn the next technology and find a way forward.

Through all of it, my goal has stayed the same: make other people's lives easier by building tools that help growth and production instead of getting in the way.

These days I've gone back to my roots. I'm farming again and making products to sell at local markets. That's where InfiniHerd and InfiniTrack came from: I needed them myself. It's also why I build software for other small businesses. I know what it's like when every hour counts.
