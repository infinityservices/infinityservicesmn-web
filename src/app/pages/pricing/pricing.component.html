How I schedule

  • I reply to new requests within 2 business days.
  • Quick fixes usually take 2–5 business days, and small tasks 1–2 weeks.
  • I take on at most two jobs at a time, so each one gets real attention.
  • I'm not an on-call or emergency service. November and December are my busiest months at markets and fairs, so I don't start new jobs then, but I'm glad to put you on the list for January.

Paying

  • Jobs under $500: invoiced when the work is done, due in 15 days.
  • Jobs $500 and up: half to start, half on delivery.
  • Anything outside the quote gets a quick new quote before I do it.
  • 14 days of free fixes for anything I delivered that isn't working right.
  • Hosting, licenses, and other services stay in your own accounts. I'll help you set them up.
  • The code is yours once the final invoice is paid.

Nonprofits

Registered 501(c)(3) nonprofits get 20% off. I also set aside some time each year to donate to causes I care about, so it never hurts to ask.
